About

Sultan Mohammad Haidari

Urban Planner & GIS Analyst

BSc in Urban Planning · Kabul University · 2026

I am an urban planning graduate specializing in the application of GIS, remote sensing, and spatial data science to urban challenges. My work focuses on urban heat islands, land-use and land-cover change, natural hazard assessment, and the integration of Python and AI into evidence-based planning workflows.

My current research — a monograph on Kabul's Urban Heat Island — investigates the relationship between urban growth, land surface temperature, and environmental resilience, combining satellite imagery, spatial modelling, and computational analysis.
